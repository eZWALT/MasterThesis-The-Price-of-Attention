## What “slow-power response” is

Not a fifth finding and not the confirmatory pair.

On Dataset B at 4 s · ICA, six exploratory cells are Holm
\(p<.05\). **Five of them are one column: explicit-early.**

| Measure | \(M\) | \(d_z\) | Holm \(p\) |
|---|---|---|---|
| global \(\delta\) | \(+4.60\) dB | 0.88 | .007 |
| global \(\theta\) | \(+1.68\) dB | 0.74 | .023 |
| relative \(\delta\) | \(+0.19\) | 0.93 | .004 |
| relative \(\alpha\) | \(-0.04\) | \(-0.73\) | .026 |
| relative \(\beta\) | \(-0.09\) | \(-0.77\) | .018 |

\(\delta\) is 0.5–4 Hz and \(\theta\) is 4–8 Hz: the slow end of
the 0.5–40 Hz band-pass. Relative powers share that denominator,
so they are compositional. A threefold rise in absolute delta
(\(+4.60\) dB) **must** lift relative delta and depress relative
alpha and beta. Those five cells are one spectral tilt toward
low frequency in the first 4 s after a labelled banner becomes
visible. That is the **slow-power response**.

The sixth Holm cell (implicit-late relative gamma, \(d_z=-0.69\))
is **not** part of that event. Scalp 30–40 Hz, no absolute-gamma
twin. Weakest cell. Do not fold it into the slow-power sentence.

## Spoken narrative (Discussion, not Results)

1. A labelled early banner is registered in the first 4 s as a
   broadband low-frequency rise.
2. An implicit mention in a streaming reply is not.
3. That is \(\lambda\), not a sustained advertisement-versus-none
   state (Dataset A any-ad is still Holm-null).
4. Preferred readings of the rise are dull: visual-onset
   transient, or saccade/blink. Against a cognitive load
   reading: global \(\theta\) moved, Fz \(\theta\) did not.
   Against attention-capture: posterior \(\alpha\) did not go
   down.
5. Not created by ICA: no-ICA dilutes it.
6. Design fact, not a liking claim: the explicit format costs a
   registered onset; the implicit format is built to avoid it.
   That is the price-of-attention line on this slide.

## What it implies (say this)

The five cells are one tilt. The implication is **not** “ads
change the brain” and **not** “they paid attention / they
engaged.” Confirmatory Fz \(\theta\) and posterior \(\alpha\)
did not move **in that Dataset B cell**. FAA, Pope, and Kislov
did not move. Dataset A any-ad and format did not move.

What follows is a **serving** claim about \(\lambda\):

- A labelled early banner is a discrete visual object. In the
  first 4 s the spectrum tilts slow. The insertion is
  **registered**.
- An implicit mention arrives inside a reply that is already
  streaming. The same measures do not move (\(|d_z|\le 0.37\)).
  That insertion is **not** registered this way.
- So the two formats are not interchangeable at the moment of
  insertion. Dataset A any-ad still says advertisement versus
  none is interchangeable as a neighbourhood-of-onset state.
- Preferred mechanism is dull (onset transient / saccade), not
  load and not gaze-capture. Still enough for \(\pi\): if you
  serve explicit, you pay an immediate neural transient; if you
  serve implicit, you do not get that registration. Notice and
  trust are the other price, and they are not estimated yet.

## Do not say (31 August, spoken line rejected)

Walter tried: tilt toward low frequency \(\Rightarrow\) active
processing \(\Rightarrow\) the ad is registered **before the
actual text**.

**Do not say that.** Two independent failures:

1. **Active processing is the wrong read of a slow tilt.**
   Awake \(\delta/\theta\) up + relative \(\beta\) down is not
   the engagement signature. Pope is \(\beta/(\alpha+\theta)\);
   this tilt is the other way. Fz \(\theta\) (effort / WM) is
   Holm-null on that cell. The locked readings are onset
   transient or saccade/blink (`sec:disc-eeg`).
2. **“Before the actual text” is the wrong cell.** Dataset B
   \(t=0\) is visual onset, not “they read it.” Explicit
   (the cell that lights up): reply is **already on screen**,
   then \(+0.49\) s, then the banner is painted.
   `eeg/2026-08-20-what-dataset-b-onset-is.md`. Implicit
   (inject \(+1.57\) s, during streaming, before the reply
   marker) is the “before the finished text” timing — and
   that column is Holm-null.

Keep: low-frequency tilt; the banner is **registered as an
onset**; implicit is not; that is \(\lambda\), not a whole-
condition difference. Drop: active processing; before the text.
